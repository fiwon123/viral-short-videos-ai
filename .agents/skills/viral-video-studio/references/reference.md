Use embedded captions if present. Use available audio transcription or listen to verify lines and pacing. Mark which of audio, visuals, and captions were inspected. For links, use the accessible public player and captions. If the player or audio is inaccessible and the full breakdown depends on it, request the local file and complete the supported part.

Group timecoded observations into scenes and shots. Verify cut candidates on both sides, including changes caused by overlays, reframing, and fast motion. Note opening and closing composition, object positions, camera or subject movement, on-screen text, speech timing, sound and music changes, and transition. Separate observed behavior from inferred tools, source assets, and performance causes. Identify the hook, setup, escalation, payoff, and exit.
