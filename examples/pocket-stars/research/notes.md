# Why this example works

- One verb carries the whole story: Pip catches. Every shot either sets it up, delivers it or brands it.
- The star is the only warm thing in a cool night, so the eye follows it from the corner to Pip's hand.
- The hand meets the star through `ik` on the star layer, so retiming the fall never breaks the catch.
- Depth comes from three hill layers at different `z` under a slow camera drift, not from extra art.
- The 3D end card repeats the star and the colours, so the brand reads as the same world.
- `make motion-report` was clean before the render: twinkles are staggered, and the reach starts once the
  star is within arm's length.
